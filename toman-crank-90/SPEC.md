# Toman Deep Crank 90 — 4 m class (Malaysia)

Target: Toman / giant snakehead (Channa micropeltes) in reservoirs and timber lakes (Kenyir, Temenggor, Pedu, etc.).
Floating deep diver. Cast and crank it down to the 3.5–4 m zone, or troll it a little deeper.

Units are mm. Axes: X runs nose (0) to tail (90), Y is width, Z is up. The bill sticks out forward of the nose (negative X).

## Body
- Length 90 mm (134 mm overall including the bill). Height 28 mm, width 20 mm.
- The fattest point is at 35 % of the length, with a blunt nose. That gives a wide, thumping wobble that toman respond to in stained water.
- The flanks are slightly boxy (superellipse n = 2.4), which adds flash and "hunting".
- Volume: body 26.6 cm³, bill 3.1 cm³, so it displaces about 29.7 g in fresh water.

## Bill (what sets the depth)
- 50 mm exposed, 26 mm wide, round nose, angled 20° below the body axis.
- The line tie sits on the bill, 14 mm from the root (≈ X -10, Z -11).
- Tuning:
  - Flatter angle (15°) = deeper and tighter action.
  - Steeper angle (25°+) = shallower and wider action.
  - A longer bill (55 mm) adds about 0.5 m of depth.

## Weight / buoyancy target
- Finished weight 24–26 g, so it floats slowly with 4–5 g of reserve buoyancy. It backs out of timber when you pause.
- Ballast goes low and forward of centre, around X 36, Z -6. Use steel or tungsten balls (e.g. 2–3 × 6 mm). That puts the centre of gravity under the centre of buoyancy, so the lure stays upright at speed.
- Check this in LureForge's float/sink analysis after you add the hooks and rings. Hooks alone add about 3–4 g.

## Hardware (toman will crush weak gear)
- Run a **through-wire** harness, 1.0–1.2 mm stainless, from the line tie to the belly hanger to the tail hanger. Don't rely on printed eyes.
- Belly hanger ≈ X 40, Z -12. Tail hanger ≈ X 88.5, Z 0.
- Hooks: #1–1/0 4X-strong trebles, or heavy inline singles. Split rings #5–6 heavy duty.
- Leader: 40–60 lb fluorocarbon or a short wire trace. Toman teeth cut line.

## Line vs depth (estimate — test before you trust it)
| Line | Approx. max depth when cast |
|---|---|
| PE 1.5–2 + leader | ~4–4.5 m |
| PE 3 + leader | ~3.5–4 m |
| PE 4+ / trolling 30 m back | trolled deeper than cast; PE4 casting loses depth |

## Colours that usually work for toman
Tea-coloured water: firetiger, chartreuse/orange belly, or red head/white.
Clear water: black/gold, or a natural haruan/sebarau pattern.
